# Israeli landscape

**Definition.** The Israeli institutions, companies and researchers behind the topics of this
manual.

**Why custody cares.** Israel founded many of the MPC custody companies, its researchers wrote
several of the protocols the demo teaches, and its central bank and exchange have run CBDC and
tokenised-bond experiments.

- **Public sector.** Bank of Israel: digital shekel preliminary design (March 2025), launch
  decision expected after end-2026; Projects Sela (with the HKMA) and Icebreaker (with Norges Bank
  and Sveriges Riksbank), both concluded 2023. Capital Market, Insurance and Savings Authority:
  licenses crypto service providers; approved Bits of Gold's BILS shekel stablecoin. Israel
  Securities Authority: token offerings. Project Eden (Ministry of Finance and TASE, 2022 to 2023):
  a dummy government bond (ERC-1155) settled against a shekel token (ERC-20), with VMware and
  Fireblocks.
- **Companies.** Fireblocks; Curv (PayPal, 2021); Unbound Security (Coinbase, January 2022); GK8
  (Celsius, 2021; Galaxy Digital, 2022); Fordefi (Paxos, November 2025); Sodot (MoonPay, April
  2026); StarkWare; Bits of Gold.
- **Researchers.** Shamir (Weizmann; secret sharing); Lindell (Bar-Ilan, Coinbase; two-party
  ECDSA); Hazay (Bar-Ilan; MPC); Makriyannis and Peled (Fireblocks; CGGMP); Pinkas (Bar-Ilan; MPC,
  private set intersection); Ishai (Technion; MPC-in-the-head); Ben-Sasson (StarkWare; STARKs);
  Sompolinsky and Zohar (Hebrew University; GHOST).

**In the demo.** Fireblocks' CGGMP co-authors and Lindell 2017 are chapter 2's protocols; Project
Eden is chapter 8's DvP cell with real participants.

**In the manual.** Chapter 8, "Israel".

# Israeli landscape

**In one sentence.** Israel founded many of the MPC custody companies, its researchers wrote several
of the protocols the demo teaches, and its central bank and stock exchange have run CBDC and
tokenised-bond experiments.

## The problem

An engineer working on custody in Israel meets the same names repeatedly: in the protocols, in the
companies that build on them, and in the public projects. This entry gathers them in one place.

## The idea

- **Public sector.** Bank of Israel: digital shekel preliminary design (March 2025), launch decision
  expected after end-2026; Projects Sela (with the HKMA) and Icebreaker (with Norges Bank and Sveriges
  Riksbank), both concluded 2023. Capital Market, Insurance and Savings Authority: licenses crypto
  service providers; approved Bits of Gold's BILS shekel stablecoin. Israel Securities Authority:
  token offerings. Project Eden (Ministry of Finance and TASE, 2022 to 2023): a dummy government bond
  (ERC-1155) settled against a shekel token (ERC-20), with VMware and Fireblocks; ERC-20 and ERC-1155
  are Ethereum's standard token interfaces.
- **Companies.** Fireblocks; Curv (PayPal, 2021); Unbound Security (Coinbase, January 2022); GK8
  (Celsius, 2021; Galaxy Digital, 2022); Fordefi (Paxos, November 2025); Sodot (MoonPay, April 2026);
  StarkWare; Bits of Gold.
- **Researchers.** Shamir (Weizmann; secret sharing); Lindell (Bar-Ilan, Coinbase; two-party ECDSA);
  Hazay (Bar-Ilan; MPC); Makriyannis and Peled (Fireblocks; CGGMP); Pinkas (Bar-Ilan; MPC, private set
  intersection); Ishai (Technion; MPC-in-the-head); Ben-Sasson (StarkWare; STARKs); Sompolinsky and
  Zohar (Hebrew University; GHOST).

## Why custody cares

Most of the Israeli custody companies were bought by larger platforms between 2021 and 2026, which
shows custody technology being sold as a component of exchanges, payment firms and stablecoin issuers.

## In the demo

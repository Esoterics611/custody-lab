# Wholesale CBDC: Agorá and mBridge

**Definition.** Central bank money in token form, held only by banks, used to settle interbank
and cross-border payments. Project Agorá puts tokenised reserves and tokenised commercial bank
deposits on one programmable platform, each central bank keeping control of its own money.
mBridge is a shared ledger on which participating central banks issue wholesale CBDC.

**Why custody cares.** Wholesale CBDC is the safest cash leg for DvP. The securities leg is still
a key held by a custodian (chapters 2 and 3).
